**What it shows, the keeper's reading:** all seven spheres occur in every genre; the false hero is rare everywhere (2% to 16% of stories), commonest in the tales. A hero exposed or punished: 22% of tragedies for both transcribers, 7% to 22% elsewhere. The donor who is also the helper, Propp's own example of one character in two spheres, is the commonest combination in the tales. A third to two fifths of characters act only outside the spheres, which include no preparatory function; not yet read further.
