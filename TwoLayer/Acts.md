# Acts and relations

**The act layer and the relation layer of the two-layer test (`TwoLayerProtocol.md`), frozen with it.**
The acts say what is done, whoever does it. The relations say how the doer stands to the one it is done to, without naming a hero.

## Acts

| Act | What happens |
|---|---|
| leave | Someone goes away from home, or from those who depend on them. |
| return | Someone comes back home. |
| forbid | Someone forbids something, or orders it. |
| disobey | Someone breaks a prohibition, or fails to do what was ordered. |
| seek-information | Someone tries to find something out. |
| reveal-information | Someone tells, shows or lets slip what another wants to know. |
| deceive | Someone lies, disguises themselves, or tricks another. |
| be-taken-in | Someone believes a deception and acts on it. |
| harm | Someone strikes, kills, seizes, steals, imprisons, curses or drives out another, or destroys what is theirs. |
| want | Someone lacks something, or wishes for it. |
| plead | Someone begs, or asks for help or mercy. |
| threaten | Someone threatens harm, or demands something under threat. |
| send | Someone sends another on an errand, or calls someone to come. |
| decide | Someone decides or agrees to act. |
| test | Someone tests, questions or challenges another, who may then be helped. |
| respond | Someone answers a test or a request: helps, refuses, passes or fails. |
| give | Someone gives, pays, sells or grants something. |
| receive | Someone gets, finds or takes something useful. |
| guide | Someone leads, carries or shows the way. |
| fight | Someone fights or competes with another. |
| win | Someone defeats another, or wins a contest. |
| lose | Someone is defeated, or fails at what they attempted. |
| mark | Someone is marked, wounded, or given a token they will be known by. |
| repair | A harm or a lack is undone: the captive freed, the lost thing recovered, the dead revived, the want met. |
| pursue | Someone chases another. |
| escape | Someone escapes from danger, or is rescued from it. |
| arrive-unknown | Someone arrives without being recognized. |
| claim-falsely | Someone claims a deed or a right that is not theirs. |
| set-task | Someone sets another a hard task. |
| do-task | Someone carries out a hard task. |
| recognize | Someone is recognized, or recognizes another. |
| expose | Someone is exposed, accused or blamed. |
| transform | Someone changes appearance or shape, or is given a new appearance. |
| punish | Someone is punished, or pardoned. |
| marry | Someone marries, or is promised in marriage. |
| lament | Someone grieves aloud over a loss. |
| other | An act that matters to the story but that none of the acts above describes. |

## Relations

The relation is how the one who acts stands to the one acted on, as the story presents them at that moment.

| Relation | Meaning |
|---|---|
| kin | Parent, child, sibling or other blood relation. |
| spouse | Husband, wife, bride, groom or suitor. |
| above | The one who acts rules, owns, commands or employs the other. |
| below | The one who acts serves, obeys or is ruled by the other. |
| ally | Friends, companions, or on the same side. |
| enemy | Opponents, rivals, or on opposite sides. |
| stranger | No tie before this event. |
| divine | One is a god, spirit or supernatural being, and the other is not. |
| self | The act is done to oneself. |
| none | No one is acted on. |
