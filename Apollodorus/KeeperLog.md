## Transcriber B

**b01: a new move at each new legend without a villainy or lack.**
In episodes that bundle several legends under one figure (L1-015, L1-016), the transcriber opened a move at each legend's first event, a convention of its own; the rules open a move only at a villainy or lack.

**b01 and b03: notation variants, read by the keeper as the rules' notation.**
Variety numbers were written in plain digits (A15), the minus as a superscript minus (E⁻), and C↑ as one sign.
`scoreApollodorus.py` reads these as the rules' notation, and C↑ as C and ↑, as Propp writes it in his own analyses; the reading changes no judgment.

**b03: each labor ordered by Eurystheus opens a move.**
The transcriber treated each order as opening a new move in place of a villainy or lack, which it reports as its own extension of the rule.
Move numbering restarts in each episode, as the rules require, even where a connected episode's move is still open.
