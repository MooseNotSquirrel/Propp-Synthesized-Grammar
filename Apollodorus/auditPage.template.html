One hundred entries drawn at random from the two transcriptions, mixed, with nothing to say which transcriber wrote which. For each, read the section and decide whether the quoted words say what the symbol claims.

How to judge an entry

Supported: the words show the act the symbol names, done and undergone by the figures named, read as Propp defines the function.

Not supported: the words do not show that act, or show it with the wrong figures, or the symbol names a function the act plainly is not.

Unsure: the reading is defensible but doubtful, or turns on something the section does not settle.

Judge the reading of the text only. Not whether the tale fits Propp, not whether you would have chosen differently among readings that are both defensible, and not what other tellings of the myth say.

Keys: S supported, N not supported, U unsure, arrow keys to move. Each answer saves as you give it, and you can change it.
