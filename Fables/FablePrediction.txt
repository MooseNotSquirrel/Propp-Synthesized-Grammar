FablePrediction.txt -- the held-out test of the fable grammar, frozen before
the held-out fables are drawn, listed or transcribed.

THE CLAIM, speculation and not Propp's: Aesop's fables follow a grammar of
their own, built from Propp's functions in an order of their own: a fable is
one or more episodes, each opening on a want (or, first, on a lead, a scheme
or a harm) and running on through a scheme, a result and a close, in that
order. Its kernel is the villain's want, the trick and the victim's
complicity, then the harm.

THE GRAMMAR: FableEBNF01.txt, written by fableGrammar.py from the order the
drawn sample's fables follow (the Aesop repository's keeper/FableShapes.txt,
both transcribers), and committed with this file. It is not changed after
this commit.

THE TRAINING FIGURES, on the drawn sample of 100 fables (versions of two or
more functions marked Y, whole fable, moves ignored), measured before this
file was written:
                   fable grammar                 v46, free move breaks
  transcriber A    74.7% real, 43.1% shuffled    63.7% real, 43.8% shuffled
                   gap +31.6                     gap +20.0
  transcriber B    75.9% real, 42.1% shuffled    65.5% real, 43.7% shuffled
                   gap +33.7                     gap +21.8
The grammar was drawn from these fables, so they overstate it.

THE HELD-OUT FABLES: 100 drawn by fableDraw.py with random.Random(3401) from
the 210 fables of Fables.txt in neither the drawn sample (Sample.txt) nor the
pilot (Pilot.txt) of the Aesop test, in batches of 25 in drawn order sorted.
A fable whose event list names no events is dropped, as F261 was.

THE INSTRUMENT, unchanged: AesopRules.md as the Aesop test used it, with its
definitions card and pilot amendments; the event-listing prompt's rules word
for word; two fresh listers on Opus, each a half of the fables; transcriber
A on Opus and B on Sonnet, blind to each other; the event lists merged as
events/Notes.md records the first merge. The transcription runs in a new
repository holding only the transcriber half, so that neither the grammar,
this file nor the Aesop result is within a transcriber's reach.

THE STREAMS: blockStream.py's, as for the training figures: each version's
functions marked Y, in the order of its Notes.txt, moves ignored.

THE MEASURES, per transcriber, over the held-out versions of two or more
functions marked Y:
  acceptance   the share FableEBNF01.txt accepts at `fable`;
  gap          acceptance minus the mean acceptance of the same streams
               shuffled in place, 1000 shuffles, seed 46;
  v46's gap    the same for v46 with +tragedy,reversal, a version passing
               when its stream splits into consecutive stretches accepted
               at `move`, the first also at `tale` (fableGrammar.py's
               v46_ok, the reading most favorable to Propp).

THE CLAIM HOLDS if, for BOTH transcribers:
  1. acceptance is at least 65%;
  2. the gap is at least 20 points;
  3. the gap exceeds v46's gap.
It fails otherwise. The thresholds are set below the training figures, which
overstate the grammar: 65% is ten points under training acceptance, and 20
points is about two thirds of the training gap.

REPORTED, NOT JUDGED: the same measures by length band (2, 3-4, 5+); the
block streams of blockStream.py for the held-out fables, all three
readings, against their shuffles; agreement between the transcribers.
