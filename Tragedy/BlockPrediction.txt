BlockPrediction.txt -- what the block analysis should show on the sealed
tragedies, frozen 2026-09-30, before any of P05-P32 is read by it.

WHEN IT RUNS: once, when the seal on P05-P32 is lifted as TragedyProtocol.md
provides (after the tragedy block grammar is frozen), with blockStream.py as
committed with this file; its OPEN list is then widened to the plays run, and
nothing else is changed.
WHAT IT READS: every play of P05-P32 with both transcriptions, both
transcribers, the three readings; baseline 200 shuffles, seed 46, as for
P01-P04.

THE FINDINGS IT TESTS come from P01-P04 alone (BlockPatterns.txt), read in the
light of Blocks/DefinitionCheck.md: most blocks' order is fixed by the card's
definitions, so order inside a block is tested only on the free blocks, and
the rest is a test of co-occurrence and adjacency.

PREDICTIONS, for EACH transcriber:
  1. Adjacency. In the blocks reading and in the strict reading, the share
     of functions inside blocks exceeds the shuffled mean, with at most 10
     of 200 shuffles at or above the real.
  2. The tragedy's blocks. complication and RuleViolation each occur,
     forward, more often than their shuffled means, in the blocks reading.
  3. Order in the free blocks. Pooled over StruggleAndOutcome, FraudPosture,
     ReturnJourney and Ordeal in the blocks reading, forward occurrences
     exceed reversed ones, and the reversed count is at or below its
     shuffled mean. If the pooled forward and reversed counts together are
     under 10, prediction 3 is reported as UNDECIDED, not as held or failed.
REPORTED, NOT PREDICTED: the reversed counts of the definitional blocks, which
check the instrument rather than the stories (DefinitionCheck.md), and all
other block counts.

A prediction holds only if it holds for both transcribers; each is reported
on its own, with no overall verdict.
