blocks in an order of its own, a strong preference, as the fables do; and
that order is closer to the plays than Propp's own order is.

THE GRAMMAR, IN SCORED FORM: TragedyOrder01.txt, written by
tragedyGrammar.py fit from P01-P04, both transcribers: each block or bare

the fable test found between-block order a preference and named a scored
model the right instrument (tragedyGrammar.py's header). This replaces,
for tragedy, the yes-or-no grammar TragedyProtocol.md speaks of: a
departure in form, logged in KeeperLog.md.
