A violation, a complicity, a victory or a solution is not a kind of act a reader names; it is whatever act fulfils or breaks the first half. Propp's pairs are relations between acts, the second defined by the first, and a list of acts made them look like pairs of act types. The one structure readers given no units find for themselves is the exchange: a request or an offer and its answer, most often a refusal.
