# The tales chosen, 25 from each source (recorded 2026-10-28, before any tale was read)

**Chosen at the owner's word** by the frozen rule of `CrossCultureProtocol.md` section 2: each source's tales in the order of its table of contents, the first 25 that are narratives, lists and notes passed over. The choice was made from the four tables of contents and the titles alone. No tale's text was opened.

Each table of contents was read from the online text (`WebFetch`, asked for the contents list only). Dorsey's comes from an OCR scan whose spelling and page numbers are approximate. They are to be checked against the page images when the event-list stage is set up, without reading the tales.
