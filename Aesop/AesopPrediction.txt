# PART 2. THE THRESHOLDS
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
# The claim: short tales are samples of Propp's production rules. It holds
# only if all three measures are met on both transcriptions, A and B, on the
# primary derivation (entries marked Y only; doubtful entries kept).
#   COVERAGE  at least 60% of events have an entry that is a function marked Y.
#   ORDER     within fables, at most 15% of ordered pairs of functions run
#             backward against Propp's numbering (beta 1 ... W 31, A and a 8;
#             the Russian corpus: 1.5%), and at least 20 points fewer than
#             the same entries shuffled within each fable (200 shuffles,
#             random.Random(46)).
#   PAIRS     every one of Propp's pairs (interdiction-violation,
#             reconnaissance-delivery, trickery-complicity, test-reaction,
#             struggle-victory, pursuit-rescue, task-solution, branding-
#             recognition, false claim-exposure, villainy or lack-liquidation,
#             departure-return) whose first function occurs at least 10 times
#             in a transcription is followed by its second within the fable
#             at least 20 points more often than when shuffled.
# Reported and not judged: the share of entries marked N; the share of
# fables holding a villainy or lack followed by its liquidation; ProppEBNF46.txt
# acceptance at `move`, as shipped and with +tragedy,reversal, against the
# opener-fixed shuffle by length band; agreement between A and B.
#
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
# PART 3. THE KEEPER'S GUESSES, FROZEN WITH THE THRESHOLDS
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
# Guesses, not thresholds; recorded so that surprise can be measured.
#   Coverage will fall short of 60%: fables turn on speech, argument and
#   boasting that no function names; about 50%.
#   Order will be met: when fables use Propp's functions, they will mostly use
#   them in his order; about 10% of pairs backward.
#   Trickery-complicity and struggle-victory will reach ten occurrences and
#   hold; few other pairs will reach ten.
#   The share of entries marked N will be low, under 15%.
#   So the claim will fail on coverage alone, with order and pairs holding.
