# source. A test binds from its step onward; runSteps.py runs the tests for
# the steps built so far and counts the rest as pending.

# commented out with a dated line saying why, and its correction is added

# Tokens:   v44's terminals, with up and down for Propp's arrows and the
#           Greek glyphs for the preparatory functions. Varieties and signs
#           are dropped: the tests are about order, not variety.
