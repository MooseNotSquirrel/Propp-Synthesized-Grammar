DIAGNOSTIC, REPORTED AND NOT JUDGED: where a candidate fell on an event that had a function in the original transcriptions, which function. Begging displaced the lack a 40 times; deceiving displaced trickery η 30 times and complicity θ 13; accusing displaced exposure Ex 11 times; asking and learning displaced mediation B, lack, reconnaissance ε, the test D and delivery ζ; refusing displaced the hero's reaction E and the agent F; oaths displaced F, a and W.
WHAT IT MEANS, the keeper's reading: the candidates are not functions Propp's list lacks. They are the acts underneath his functions: a plea is how a lack or a call for help is told, a deception is the act of η, an accusation is the act of an exposure, a refusal is a negative reaction E. Given a role-free name for the act, the transcribers often prefer it to Propp's role-bound function, which is why the candidates take his events. That is evidence for the two-layer catalog of acts and roles proposed in PreliminaryFindings, section 4: an act layer under Propp's functions, not new functions beside them. Threats and demands is the one act that recurs, is agreed on, and mostly fills events Propp leaves empty.
