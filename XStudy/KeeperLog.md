A CRITERION THAT DID NO WORK: every agreed event carries a link in at least one transcription, so criterion 3 (a consequence) passed every kind. The transcribers fill the link field for nearly every entry they mark as mattering; it does not separate acts with consequences from acts without.
NOTED FOR THE TEST: several candidates resemble Propp's functions (deceiving, attacking, sending, gifts, proving identity, asking and learning). These events were left X by both transcribers, presumably because the act did not fit the function as the card defines it (performer, position, or purpose). Stage 5's second test, that a candidate take up events left X and not Propp's, is what will tell.
