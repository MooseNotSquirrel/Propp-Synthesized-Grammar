# Candidate acts: the card entries for the X study's test

**Frozen 2026-10-06, after the count (`CountRun.txt`) and before any test transcription.** Written from the grouper's definitions in `group/Kinds.txt`, word for word; only the symbol and the rule of use are added.

**The rule of use, added to the card for the test:** write a candidate act only for an event in which none of the functions above it on the card fits, as those functions are defined there. An event that a function fits takes the function, never a candidate. An event that neither a function nor a candidate fits is X, as before.

| Symbol | Act | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| xArr | arriving on the scene | Someone arrives or comes upon others, bringing a new person, news or force into play and opening the next turn of events. |
| xBeg | begging for help or mercy | Someone pleads, begs or cries out for help, mercy or protection, putting the hearer under pressure to decide whether to give it. |
| xDec | deceiving with lies or disguise | Someone lies, feigns or disguises the truth, so that others act on a false picture of things. |
| xThr | threats and demands | Someone threatens harm or demands something under threat, forcing the other to yield, resist or flee. |
| xAtt | attacking and killing | Someone strikes, seizes, fights or kills, causing harm that changes who is alive, free or in power. |
| xOat | oaths, pacts and marriages | Someone swears, promises, allies or gives in marriage, binding people to each other and to future duties. |
| xBoa | boasts, feats and contests | Someone boasts, shows off a feat or issues or accepts a challenge, setting up a trial of strength or skill. |
| xRes | resolving on revenge or harm | Someone resolves, vows or plans to do harm or take revenge, launching a course of action against a target. |
| xAcc | accusing and blaming | Someone accuses, blames or rebukes another, turning opinion or guilt against him and provoking a quarrel. |
| xPro | proving who someone is | Someone offers or finds a token, memory or sign of identity, leading toward recognition of kin. |
| xAsk | asking and learning | Someone asks questions, seeks news or receives a report, gaining knowledge that directs the next move. |
| xGif | gifts, payment and trade | Someone gives, sells, buys, rewards or demands payment, shifting goods and obligations between people. |
| xSen | sending and summoning | Someone sends another on an errand or calls someone to come, bringing a person or message where it is needed. |
| xHid | hiding and watching | Someone hides, keeps watch or goes unnoticed, keeping a secret safe for now or learning one. |
| xRef | refusing and rejecting | Someone refuses a request, offer or claim, blocking what was proposed and forcing another course. |
| xFai | failing to act | Someone sleeps, delays, waits or stays away instead of acting, leaving a deed unclaimed or giving others time. |
