WHAT IT MEANS, the keeper's reading: the tricks are real, every one; what is
not always real is the complicity. Both transcribers write θ for the
victim's RESPONSE to a trick, yielding or refusing, though the card defines
θ only as yielding and Propp has no refusing form. Because both do it, and
in the same fables, it inflates the DeceptionTrap counts and the
transcribers' agreement on them alike. So the block the fables carry is,
read faithfully, the trick and the victim's response, in about two thirds
of cases the response Propp names (complicity) and in about a third its
refusal. The refusals are the fables whose trick fails, which ties this to
the trick's second outcome found in the held-out fables.
