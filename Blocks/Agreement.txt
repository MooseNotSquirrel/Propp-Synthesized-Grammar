Agreement.txt -- risk check 3, frozen 2026-09-30 before the code is written:
do the two transcribers find the same blocks in the same story?

THE WORRY: the block effect is pooled over stories and transcribers. If the
blocks were artifacts of each transcriber's labelling, A and B would each
show blocks, but in different places.

THE MEASURE: each version is run through blockStream.py's transform, and each
block is taken with the set of event numbers its functions come from. A block
AGREES when the other transcriber's version of the same story has a block of
the same entry, reversed or not alike, whose events overlap it. Agreement is
the share of all blocks, A's and B's together, that agree (a Dice share).
Versions are matched by identifier; a single-hero story labelled with "/1"
by one transcriber and without it by the other is the same version.

THE COMPARISON: A's blocks as transcribed against B's with B's functions
marked Y shuffled in place within each version, 200 shuffles, seed 46; the
agreement computed the same way each time.

THE CORPORA AND READINGS: the training fables, the held-out fables and
tragedy P01-P04; the blocks and strict readings. The sealed plays are not
read.

THE DECISION RULE, per corpus and reading: the transcribers AGREE BEYOND
CHANCE if the real agreement exceeds the shuffled mean with at most 10 of 200
shuffles at or above it; otherwise they do NOT. Reported with the agreement
by entry for the entries with at least five blocks; no overall verdict.

(The phrase "reversed or not alike" above means: the reversal mark is ignored
in matching, a reversed and a forward block of the same entry alike.)

RESULT, 2026-09-30 (blockAgreement.py, output AgreementRun.txt):
  training fables: AGREE BEYOND CHANCE in both readings (70.9% against
    59.9%, 0 of 200; strict 66.7% against 55.2%, 1 of 200).
  held-out fables: AGREE BEYOND CHANCE in both (59.4% against 54.8%, 5 of
    200; strict 67.9% against 55.0%, 0 of 200).
  tragedy P01-P04: blocks AGREE BEYOND CHANCE (75.3% against 65.6%, 6 of
    200); strict DO NOT by the rule (62.5% against 49.9%, 16 of 200), on only
    40 blocks in four plays.
  By entry, DeceptionTrap agrees best, 78% to 88% of its blocks in all four
  fable cells; complication about 57% to 81%. The shuffled agreement is high
  (50% to 66%) because the match is lenient, any shared event, and
  complication is common; the margins are the finding, not the levels.
