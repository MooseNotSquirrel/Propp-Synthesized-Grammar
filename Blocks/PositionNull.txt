PositionNull.txt -- risk check 2, frozen 2026-09-30 before the code is written:
do Propp's blocks exceed a comparison that keeps each function's habits of
position?

THE WORRY: the plain shuffle scrambles every function freely, but stories
have strong habits of position (a want early, a harm late). Two functions
that tend to fall in the same part of a story will often sit together with
no bond between them, and the plain shuffle counts that as a block effect.

THE POSITIONAL NULL: for each shuffle of a version, every function marked Y
draws a sort key at random from the relative positions, (i + 0.5) / n, that
the same function takes in the other stories of the same corpus and
transcriber, its own story left out (all versions of the same fable or play);
a function found in no other story draws a key uniformly from 0 to 1. The
functions are sorted by their keys and put back into the version's Y
positions; transparent entries stay where they are. 200 shuffles, seed 46.

THE CORPORA: the training fables (Aesop), the held-out fables (Propp-Fables)
and tragedy P01-P04; the sealed plays are not read.

THE DECISION RULE, per corpus, transcriber and reading (blocks, strict): the
block effect SURVIVES the positional null if the real share of functions
inside blocks exceeds the positional null's mean with at most 10 of 200
shuffles at or above the real; it is WEAKENED if it exceeds the mean with
more than 10; it is GONE if it does not exceed the mean. Reported for each
cell, with the plain shuffle's figure beside it; no overall verdict.

RESULT, 2026-09-30 (positionNull.py, output PositionNullRun.txt):
  fables, training and held-out: SURVIVES in all eight cells, 0 of 200 each;
    the positional null sits within 2 points of the plain shuffle, so
    habits of position explain almost none of the fables' block effect.
  tragedy P01-P04, strict: SURVIVES for both transcribers, 6 of 200 each.
  tragedy P01-P04, blocks: WEAKENED for both, 74 and 89 of 200; the
    positional null (49.0%, 54.1%) nearly reaches the real (50.3%, 54.8%),
    so in the loose reading the tragedies' block effect is mostly habit of
    position. Only four plays feed the positions, three per story left out.
